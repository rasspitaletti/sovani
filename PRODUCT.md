# Product

Independent concept redesign of Soneva Jani (Noonu Atoll, Maldives) for the author's portfolio and Instagram Reels. Not affiliated with the resort.

- **Mode:** Experience, with a persuade close that hands off to the official Soneva channels.
- **Audience:** people reviewing the author's frontend and motion work; secondarily, travellers who should find real facts.
- **Idea:** the villas' retractable roof. The site opens as a roof opens, and the visitor sets the hour; that light carries through the page.
- **Hard limits:** no invented prices, availability, reviews or facilities; the concept notice always visible.

Assumption: inferred from the brief. The art references named in the brief are pending, so colour and type are provisional tokens in `src/app/globals.css`.
